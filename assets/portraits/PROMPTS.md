# 人物イラスト生成記録

生成方法: 組み込み image_gen。生成後、画面用に384×384 WebPへ最適化。
保存先（リポジトリ内）: assets/portraits/<ID>.webp

## young-woman

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: reusable portrait avatar for a Japanese beginner English conversation app. Create one square head-and-shoulders portrait of a friendly fictional young adult woman, age 20, warm medium skin, straight dark brown bob hair, simple cream shirt and muted teal cardigan. Soft polished editorial illustration, gently textured matte colors, clean rounded shapes, natural facial proportions, welcoming slight smile. Center the whole head and shoulders with comfortable margin, front-facing on solid pale warm ivory background. No text, letters, labels, symbols, flags, props, borders, logos or watermark. Make the character approachable for a school learning app, not infantile, not photorealistic.
